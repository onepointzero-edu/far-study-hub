## Chapter pattern

Each chapter follows the structure your own FAR 0 modules use:

1. **Teaching parts** — each isolating one idea, with one or two focused
   illustrative examples placed **before** the knowledge check.
2. **A comprehensive running case** at the end — one fact pattern carried from
   initial measurement through subsequent measurement and presentation to
   derecognition, split across gated stages so students work it progressively.

The **Leases**, **Income Tax** and **Employee Benefits** chapters implement this in
full. Still to retrofit: the **Conceptual Framework** and the **Cash** group.

## Suggested build order

**Phase 1 — Section H, the dense block** (chapters 11–15, ~7 items)

Leases, Income Tax, Employee Benefits, Interim Reporting, Operating Segments. All
five are RETAIN with complete handouts, and Leases and Income Tax carry Excel
solutions so computational answers can be checked against your own worked files.
This is the highest return per hour of anything on the list.

**Phase 2 — the rest of FAR 3** (chapters 16–19, ~4 items)

NCAHFS, Accounting Changes, Events After Reporting Period, Statement of Cash Flows.
All RETAIN, all with handouts.

**Phase 3 — FAR 0 core** (chapters 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, ~7 items)

Accounting Cycle, Cash, Receivables, Inventories, Biological Assets. All RETAIN.
Chapter 4 (Conceptual Framework) is already built and sits in this group.

**Phase 4 — the gaps that need new writing** (chapters 1, 2, 5, 10, 20)

These need content that does not exist in any handout:

- **A.1** — ISSB and PSRC (chapter 1)
- **A.2** — regulation, PRBOA, APO, sectors of practice, **functions of the SEC** (chapter 2)
- **B.1.4** — presentation and disclosure as effective communication (chapter 5)
- **D.7** — prepaid expenses as a named unit (chapter 10)
- **I.1.3** — **PFRS for Cooperatives** (chapter 20)

Do these last: they need you to draft source material first, whereas Phases 1–3 can
be built straight from existing handouts.

## The built chapter needs two additions

Chapter 4 is labelled **TOS B.1**, which is correct under 2029 numbering. Against the
2029 sub-topics it is missing:

- **B.1.4 Presentation and Disclosure** — now an explicit Conceptual Framework
  sub-topic, split between the body of the statements (1.4.1) and the notes (1.4.2).
  Your revised OBTL makes this a separate FAR 0 unit, so it becomes chapter 5 rather
  than an edit — but chapter 4 should at least point forward to it.
- **B.1.4.2** names related party disclosures, events after the reporting period,
  contingencies and significant non-financial information as note content understood
  at Framework level. Chapter 4 currently treats these as belonging elsewhere.

Note the split across your subjects: **Events After the Reporting Period is taught in
FAR 3** (chapter 18) but sits under **B.1.4.2** in the TOS. Both chapters should
cross-link so students see the connection.
